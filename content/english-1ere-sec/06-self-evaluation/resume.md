# 📜 Summary: Self Evaluation

- **The question builder** — one line per helper: **WH word + am/is/are/was/were + subject** for a state (_What **is** your strong point?_), **WH word + did + subject + base verb** for a finished action (_How **did** you learn that word?_), **WH word + do/does + subject + base verb** for a habit (_Which activity **do** you enjoy most?_). The short answer repeats the **helper of the question**, never the main verb (_Yes, I **did**._), and a positive one is never contracted ("Yes, I'm." ✗).
- **Saying what is easy and what is hard** — one phrase per thing you judge: difficulty (_I **found** lesson 3 fairly difficult_), length (_**too long**_), interest (_**quite interesting**_), understanding (_**most of it**_), a strong point (_I'm **good at** reading_), a difficulty (_I'm **not very good at** speaking_) — a noun or an **-ing** verb after **at**. **too** is not _very_: it means _more than I want_. With your partner you report the common pair with **we** and **our**.
- **Five ways to make a suggestion** — **Why not…?** + the **base verb** without _to_ (_Why not **read** an English story every week?_); **What about…?** + a **noun** or an **-ing** verb (_What about a **notebook**?_, _What about **listening** to songs?_); **You should…** + the base verb, the strongest advice; **You could…** + the base verb, a softer possibility; **If you…, you…**, present tense after _if_ and **will** after it (_If you **read** more, you **will** learn faster._). _Why not…?_ and _What about…?_ are friendly offers and end with a **question mark**; the same idea only changes shape between them (_Why not **listen**…?_ = _What about **listening**…?_).
- **Resolutions with will** — a decision taken now, for the future: **will + base verb** (_I **will** speak more English in class._), contracted **I'll**, negative **won't** (_I **won't** translate every single word._). Nothing comes between _will_ and the base verb ("She will reads" ✗). Pick one from the class list or add your own. _I **will** revise_ and _I'm **going to** revise_ are both correct here.
- **The model review** — two classmates interview each other, answer with short answers (_Yes, I did._ / _No, I haven't._), name a strong point and a difficulty, suggest (_Why not keep a notebook…?_, _What about testing each other…?_, _If you do that, you will remember them longer._), take a resolution (_I'll bring the first list._) and end by agreeing on what they share.
- **Planning ahead** — before lesson 10, research **Ibn Khaldun** (ask your Arabic teacher, the school or public library, a website) using six guide questions: _When **was** he born?_ · _What kind of education **did** he get?_ · _Where **did** he live mainly?_ · _What **did** he write about?_ · _What positions **did** he hold?_ · _What **is** his best known book?_ All six come out of the chapter's two formulas: **was/is + subject** for the first and the last, **did + subject + base verb** for the four in between. The questions are yours to answer, not the course's.
- **The trap wall** — _What about **going** to the library?_ (never "to go"), _**Why not try** a new method?_ (never "why not to try"), _You **should revise** more._ (**should** and **could** take the base verb alone), _I'm good at **speaking** English._ (never "at to speak"), _**If you read** more, you **will** learn._ (present after **if**, **will** in the other half).
