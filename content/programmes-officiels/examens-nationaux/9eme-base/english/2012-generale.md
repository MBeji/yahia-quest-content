## Texte support (page 1, « THE TEXT »)

- Référence imprimée : *Adapted from ‘Jane Eyre’, Charlotte Brontë* ; trois paragraphes repérés ①②③ ; environ 270 mots (une trentaine de lignes).
- Résumé : à dix ans, une orpheline vit chez sa tante, riche, dans une grande et belle maison où elle est malheureuse : ses trois cousins, plus âgés, la tiennent à l'écart et sont méchants. Elle a surtout peur de l'aîné des garçons, qui aime l'effrayer. Un après-midi, elle se cache ; une cousine la débusque et le garçon lui jette un gros livre qui la blesse à la tête. La tante, furieuse, ne remarque pas le sang et la gronde.
- Citation utile à une question (paragraphe 2) : « He could not find me at first - he was not quick or clever. »
- Texte intégral : PDF officiel, sha256 au registre.

## I. READING COMPREHENSION (6 marks)

Read the text and answer the questions.

### Comprehension questions :

1- Tick (✓) the right alternative (1 mark)
   The text is mainly about a girl who:
   A- suffered bad treatment at her aunt's. [case]
   B- left her parents to live with her aunt. [case]
   C- was treated well by her loving aunt. [case]
2- Are the following statements true or false? Tick (✓) the proper box. (1x2 = 2 marks)
   | STATEMENTS | TRUE | FALSE |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | A Jane was an orphan. | [case] | [case] |
   | B Mrs. Reed could not afford to support Jane. | [case] | [case] |
3- Circle the adjective that best describes John (1 mark)
   intelligent – aggressive – supportive – friendly
4- Complete the following sentences with two words from the text. (1x2 = 2 marks)
   Jane felt unhappy because she was always ........ by her cousins.
   Her aunt did not feel sorry for her when John ........ her with a book.

## II. LANGUAGE (8 marks)

1- Fill in the blanks with six words from the list (0.5 x 6 = 3 marks)
   holidays – favourite – divide – at – partner – for – share – sell

   Some people use the Internet to book a hotel room for their ........ or tickets for the cinema. They pay their electricity bills and even buy cars online.
