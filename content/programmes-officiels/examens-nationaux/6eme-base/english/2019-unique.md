## Texte support (« The Text », page 1)

- Référence imprimée : aucune ; deux paragraphes numérotés 1 et 2 ; environ 105 mots.
- Résumé : deux enfants d'une grande école internationale décrivent leur école (classes, salle d'informatique, cour de récréation) puis leur matière préférée, la musique : chacun joue d'un instrument et, en cours, la classe écoute des musiques du monde et chante une chanson choisie.
- Texte intégral : PDF officiel, sha256 au registre.

## I. READING COMPREHENSION (6 marks)

1. Tick (✓) the right option to complete the sentence. (1mark)
   In the music class, pupils sing...
   a) together. [case]  b) in pairs. [case]  c) in groups. [case]
2. Tick the __two__ true statements. (2 marks)
   a) Katy and Paul listen to English music. [case]
   b) The teacher writes the favourite song of his pupils on the blackboard. [case]
   c) The playground is near the computer room. [case]
   d) Paul loves playing the piano. [case]
3. Complete the sentences with __three__ words from paragraph 2. (3marks)
   Paul and his friend study at Bedford school. It's a big school where pupils come from ........ countries. The two children enjoy playing the piano and the ........ because playing music is their ........ hobby.

## II. LANGUAGE (8 marks)

1. Circle the right option. (3 marks)
   Masha is a nice little girl. ( **He / She / They** ) likes all animals, ( **because / then / but** ) her favourite one is the bear. She lives ( **in / on / at** ) a small yellow house in Russia.
2. Write the opposite of the underlined words to complete the dialogue. (2 marks)
   Teacher: Can you open the window please? It's very __cold__ ≠ ........ .
   Pupil: The window on the __right__ ≠ on the ........ ?
   Teacher: Yes, thank you.
3. Fill in the blanks with __three__ words from the box. (3 marks)
   up / animals /for / to
   Today is hot and sunny. Bill and Cindy get ........ early because their classes are going to the zoo. When they get ........ school, their friends are on the bus. Bill tells his friends about his favourite ........ .

## III. WRITING (6 marks)

1. Reorder the words to make questions. ( 2 marks)
   a) books / bag / ~~How~~ / your / have / in / many / got / you ? → How ........ ?
   b) ready / exam / ~~Are~~ / the / you / for ? → Are ........ ?
   c) watch / ~~Who~~ / game / 's / to / the / going / football ? → Who ........ ?
   d) you / do / tonight / to / ~~What~~ / going / are ? → What ........ ?
2. Answer the following questions to write a short e-mail (four sentences) to your new friend "Sally" to tell her about yourself. (4 marks)
   - How many brothers and sisters have you got? / What's your hobby?
   - What's your favourite school subject? / What's your favourite T.V programme?
