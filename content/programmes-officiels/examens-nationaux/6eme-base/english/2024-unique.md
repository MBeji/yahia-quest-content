**sometimes / cleanest / collect / but / cleaner**

Last week, we celebrated Teachers' Day. It was on February 28th. The party was enjoyable. Students ( **sings / sing / sang** ) nice songs, made videos with their teachers and offered ( **they / them / their** ) special cards with lovely messages.
