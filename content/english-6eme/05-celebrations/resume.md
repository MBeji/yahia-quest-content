# 📜 Summary: Celebrations

- **The days we celebrate** — **Children's Day** (the eleventh of January), **Mother's Day**, **Teacher's Day**, **New Year**, the **end of school year party**; to **celebrate**, a **gift**, a **wish**, **snacks**, **crackers**, a **clown**, a **show**, **magic**; people **decorate** the room, **blow up** the balloons, **put on** their best clothes, **hug** each other, **wait for** midnight, and somebody is **surprised**.
- **Rights** — **I / you / we / they have the right to** · **he / she / it has the right to**: the right to **education**, to **life**, to **clothing**, to **health**.
- **The simple past** — it tells what happened at a moment that is **over**. A regular verb takes **-ed**, and the form is the **same for every subject** (_I played_, _she played_, never _playeds_).
- **The words that call the past** — **yesterday** · **last** + a moment (last week, last night, last year) · a number + **ago** (four days ago).
- **Writing the -ed** — **+ ed** after a consonant (wait → wait**ed**, ask → ask**ed**, talk → talk**ed**) · **+ d** when the verb already ends in **e** (love → love**d**, prepare → prepare**d**, decorate → decorate**d**) · **double the consonant + ed** for a short verb in one vowel + one consonant (hug → hug**ged**, stop → stop**ped**).
- **Irregular verbs** — buy → **bought** · write → **wrote** · do → **did** · have → **had** · eat → **ate** · drink → **drank** · make → **made** · say → **said** · blow up → **blew up** · put → **put** (it does not change). Never _buyed_, never _maked_.
- **The past of be** — I / he / she / it → **was** · you / we / they → **were** (_I was surprised_, _we were at the party_).
- **Pronunciation of the final -ed** — **/t/** after a quiet sound (talked, asked, danced) · **/d/** after a sound that shakes the throat or a vowel (played, hugged, prepared, lived) · **/ɪd/** only after **t** or **d**, where a whole syllable appears (waited, counted, wanted, decorated).
