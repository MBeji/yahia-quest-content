# 📜 Summary: Entertaining Myself

- **Free time activities** — name them with the verb + **-ing**: fishing, camping, gardening, drawing, shopping, riding a bike, flying a kite, rope skipping, hide and seek, the museum.
- **Saying what you like — and why** — **like / enjoy / hate / dislike + verb + ing** (_I enjoy gardening_, never _to_ after these four verbs); **because** gives the reason, **and** joins two ideas that go together, **but** joins two that are different.
- **Adverbs of frequency** — **always** (every time), **usually** (most times), **sometimes** (not often), **never** (not one time); they stand **before the verb**, and with he / she / it the verb takes an **-s**.
- **When? on and in** — **on** + a day (on Friday, on the weekend, on Friday morning); **in** + a part of the day (in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening).
- **Suggestions** — **Let's** + bare verb (_Let's go_), **How about / What about** + verb + **ing** (_How about going?_). Answer: _Good idea!_
- **Invitations** — **Would you like + to + verb?** (_Would you like to join our team?_); answer _Yes, please._ / _Yes, I'd love to._ / _No, thanks._
- **Possessive adjectives** — my, your, his, her, its, our, their, **before the noun**; the **owner** chooses the word, not the object (_Nadia and her kite_).
- **Pronunciation** — the letter s has two sounds: hissing **/s/** (let's, cinema, restaurant) and buzzing **/z/** (zoo, museum, plays).
