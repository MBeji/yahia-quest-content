# 📜 Summary: Around the World

- **The cardinal directions** — **north** at the top of the map, **south** at the bottom, **east** on the right, **west** on the left (N, S, E, W). They describe the Earth, never the sheet of paper: _Italy is north of Tunisia._ A **country** has its own name, flag and people; to **travel** is to go far away.
- **Suggesting a journey** — **Let's** + bare verb (_Let's travel to Italy_), **How about / What about** + verb + **ing** (_How about visiting Rome?_). Answer: _Good idea!_ or _Sorry, it is too far._
- **Greetings around the world** — to **greet** is to say hello when you meet: people **shake hands** (England), give a **kiss** on each **cheek** (Tunisia, France, Spain), **bow** (Japan) or **touch** noses and foreheads (Maori, New Zealand). A good traveller uses the greeting of the country they are in.
- **The articles** — **a** before a consonant (a cape, a dancer), **an** before a vowel (an amphitheatre, an Indian), **the** before what is already known or unique in the world (the world, the north, the pyramids).
- **Travel by, travel to** — **by** + a vehicle, with no little word after it (by plane, by train, by car, by boat); **to** + a place (to Italy, to Sousse). To say which one you choose: **prefer to** + verb = **prefer** + verb + **ing** (_I prefer to travel by plane_ = _I prefer travelling by plane_). A plane is **fast**, a boat is **slow**.
- **The comparative** — two things compared: short adjective + **-er** + **than** (faster than, older than); only -r after -e (nicer), -ier after consonant + y (easier), a doubled letter after consonant-vowel-consonant (bigger). Never _more_ in front of a short adjective.
- **The superlative** — one thing above a whole group: **the** + short adjective + **-est** (the tallest, the oldest, the longest). The five monuments: the **pyramids** (Egypt, about 4500 years old), the **amphitheatre of El Jem** (Tunisia), **Big Ben** (England, 1859), the **Eiffel Tower** (France, 1889, 330 metres — the tallest of the unit) and the **Great Wall of China**.
- **Customs and habits** — a **custom** belongs to a country and comes back every year (paella with **seafood** and **pepper**, the flamenco **dancer**, the **festival** of La Tomatina where people **throw** tomatoes, the red **cape** of the **bull** ring, couscous on Friday in Tunisia); a **habit** belongs to a person. Both are told in the **simple present**.
