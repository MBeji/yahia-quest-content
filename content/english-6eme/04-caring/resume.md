# 📜 Summary: Caring

- **The words of caring** — to **care**, to **take care of**, to **help**, to **share**, to **wash the dishes**, to **tidy**, to **clean up**, to **come together**; **dirty** ≠ **tidy**, a **neighbour**, **alone**; and **each other** when the help goes both ways (_we help each other_).
- **Possessive adjectives** — my, your, his, her, its, our, their, always **before** the noun; the **owner** chooses the word (_This is **her** bag_, never _she bag_).
- **The superlative** — **the + short adjective + est** puts one above the whole group: the clean**est**, the old**est**, the young**est**; big → the big**gest** (double the consonant), happy → the happ**iest** (y becomes i). Keep it apart from the comparative **-er + than**, which compares only two.
- **Verb + er = the person** — farm → a farm**er**, build → a build**er**, teach → a teach**er**, clean → a clean**er**, garden → a garden**er**, play → a play**er**; drive → a drive**r** (it already ends in e), run → a run**ner**. But a **carpenter**, a **doctor** and a **vet** come from no verb — learn them whole.
- **The future** — **will + bare verb** for every subject (_She will help_, never _will helps_); negative **will not = won't**; short form _I'll, we'll_.
- **The imperative** — bare verb, **no subject**: _Close the door._ _Give your dog water twice a day._
- **Prohibition** — **Don't + verb**: _Don't hit your cat._ _Don't forget its water._ (never _Not close the door_).
- **Pronunciation** — you **hear** the h in help, house, home, homework, hopscotch; you **don't hear** it in hour, where, dishes, school, should.
