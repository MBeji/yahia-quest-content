# 📜 Summary: Having Fun in the Seasons

- **Four seasons, twelve months** — the **year** turns through **spring** (March, April, May), **summer** (June, July, August), **autumn** (September, October, November) and **winter** (December, January, February); after December comes January.
- **Noun + y = adjective** — cloud → **cloudy**, rain → **rainy**, wind → **windy**, snow → **snowy**; a consonant + vowel + consonant noun doubles its last letter: sun → **sunny**, fog → **foggy**.
- **What's the weather like?** — the fixed question, answered with **It's + adjective**: _It's sunny._ · _It's cold and windy._ The **sky** can be **clear**, the **air** can be **fresh**.
- **The future — will / won't** — **will** + bare verb for what will happen (_I will go to the beach_), **won't** + bare verb for what will not (_It won't rain_); **will** never changes and the verb never takes an **-s**. Future words: tomorrow, next week, next month, next year.
- **Comparing — adjective + er + than** — a short adjective takes **-er** and the second thing comes after **than**: colder than, warmer than, cooler than, longer than; and hot → **hotter** doubles its last letter.
- **Verb + ing = activity** — play → **playing**, catch → **catching**, collect → **collecting**, walk → **walking**; swim → **swimming** and run → **running** double their last letter.
- **The words of the seasons** — spring: **sky**, **clear**, **outside**, **air**, **butterfly**, **catch**, **collect**; summer: **shorts**, **swimsuit**, **beach**, **sandcastle**, to be **on holidays**; autumn: **warm**, **cold**, **windy**, **go back**, **meet**; winter: **gloves**, **socks**, **boots**, **sweater**, **fresh air**.
- **Pronunciation** — **/ð/** is the sound of **the**: the tongue goes between the teeth and the throat shakes — weather, with, them, this, mother.
