# 📜 Summary: The Future

- **Be going to — an intention** — **am / is / are + going to + base verb** for something you have decided to do: _Rania is going to become a biologist._ Never drop _is_ (_Omar going to…_ ✗).
- **Present progressive + a future word — a planned action** — **am / is / are + verb + ing + a future word** (tomorrow, next week, the coming month): _I am visiting my uncle tomorrow._ Without the future word, the same form means "now".
- **Intention or planned action?** — _going to_ + base verb → intention; am / is / are + verb + ing + future word → planned action. _We are going to Djerba next week_ is the verb _go_ → a planned action.
- **Will and won't — the future tense** — **will / won't + bare infinitive**, the same with every subject: _Cars will be cheap. They won't use petrol. Will they cost a lot?_ Never _will uses_, never _will to do_.
- **Intend to** — subject + **intend(s) to + base verb**: _Kate intends to rent a house._ Questions and negatives with do / does: _She doesn't intend to leave school._
- **Be thinking of + verb + ing** — **am / is / are thinking of + verb + ing**: _I'm thinking of studying abroad._ After **of**, the verb takes **-ing** (never _thinking to leave_).
- **Going further** — _when_ / _as soon as_ + present for the future (_I will call you when I arrive_) is beyond the 9th-grade book.
