# 📜 Summary: Present Tenses

- **Present simple** — habits and facts; subject + base verb, and **he / she / it** add **-s**: _works_, _goes_, _watches_, _studies_, _has_ (_My brother goes to school by bus._).
- **Questions and negatives** — **do / does** + base verb: _She doesn't play._ / _Does she play?_ / _What does she read?_ — the -s is on **does**, never on the verb too; **be** makes its own (_She isn't tired._).
- **Adverbs of frequency** — always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never go **before** the main verb and **after** be (_I always go…_, _He is never late._); _every day_, _on Sundays_ go at the end.
- **Present continuous** — **am / is / are + verb-ing** for an action in progress now; never the -ing form alone (_My mother is cooking._); negative _isn't / aren't_, question _Is he…? / Are they…?_
- **Simple or continuous?** — habit or fact → present simple (_every day_); now → present continuous (_now_, _at the moment_, _Look!_, _Listen!_); _these days_ = a temporary situation → continuous.
- **Stative verbs** — know, understand, like, love, want, need, prefer, have (= possess) stay in the **present simple** even now (_I know the answer._); _have_, _taste_, _think_ take -ing only when they mean an action (_I'm having lunch_, _I'm thinking about my exam_).
